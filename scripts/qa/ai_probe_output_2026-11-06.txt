Unpaid salary is a clear violation of the Code on Wages, 2019, which mandates that your wages must be paid by the 7th of the following month. Since you have a clear claim for Rs 25,000, your case is strong provided you have documentation to prove your employment and the amount due. The law is on your side here, and you do not need to accept excuses about 'cash flow' or 'company policy'.

**My view:**
This is a straightforward recovery matter. Under the current labour laws, your employer cannot withhold your salary without a valid, legally permitted reason (like a court order or specific, proven damage you caused). Since you are owed a specific amount, you have leverage to demand payment immediately. The most effective path is to create a formal paper trail that shows you are serious about escalating this to the labour authorities.

**Law on your side:**
*   **Code on Wages, 2019 (Section 17):** This law explicitly states that for monthly workers, wages must be paid before the expiry of the 7th day of the succeeding month. 
*   **Code on Wages, 2019 (Section 45):** You have the right to file a claim before the government-appointed authority to recover unpaid wages. This process is designed to be accessible to employees.
