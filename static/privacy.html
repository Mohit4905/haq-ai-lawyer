Privacy policy

Haq is a student project: a free AI legal-help assistant for everyday problems in India. Last updated 6 November 2026.

What Haq stores

  • As a guest: your cases, chats, drafts and evidence files are saved only in your own browser on this device. Nothing is kept on our server.
  • With an account: your email, first name and the profile details you choose to add (city, language, reminder setting), and your cases, chats, drafts and evidence files, stored with our database provider (Supabase). Each account can see only its own data.
  • Google sign-in: we receive only your name, email address and profile picture from Google. We do not get access to your Gmail, Drive or any other Google data.

Who processes your messages

  • To answer you, the text you type (and documents you ask Haq to read) is sent to an AI model provider (Groq or Google Gemini) for processing.
  • If you track a court case, the case number (CNR) or party name you enter is sent to eCourtsIndia to fetch the public court record.
  • If you rate a reply (👍/👎), only the rating, the message id, the AI model and the problem type are recorded in the server log. If you report a reply, the reply's text is also recorded so it can be reviewed. Your own messages are not included.
  • If you switch on email reminders, your email and deadline are used to send them through our email provider.

We do not sell your data, show ads or use your data to train AI models.

Deleting your data

You can delete any case at any time, or delete your whole account and everything in it from your profile in Haq. As a guest, clearing your browser data removes everything.

Important

Haq gives legal information, not legal advice, and is not a lawyer. Please don't share passwords, OTPs, Aadhaar or bank PINs in the chat.

Contact

For any privacy question or deletion request, write to the support email shown on the Google sign-in screen for Haq.
