Haq is a free student project. Last updated 6 November 2026.

What Haq is

Haq is an AI assistant that explains Indian law for everyday problems (deposits, consumer refunds, unpaid salary, bounced cheques, legal notices), drafts letters and notices for you to send yourself, and shows public court records. It gives legal information, not legal advice. Haq is not a lawyer or a law firm, and using it does not create a lawyer–client relationship.

Your responsibility

  • AI can make mistakes. Check every draft, date, amount and legal reference before you rely on it or send it.
  • For criminal matters, court proceedings, large amounts, or when a deadline is close, consult a lawyer. Free legal aid is available through NALSA (helpline 15100) and your District Legal Services Authority.
  • Court data comes from public eCourts records through a third-party service and may be delayed or incomplete; confirm on the official eCourts website.
  • Do not use Haq to harass anyone or to make false claims.
